## OPC Persona

ALMA is a premium Venezuelan food and beverage brand based in Toronto. The owner sells artisan drinks, baked goods, and gourmet pantry products across three lines: Beverages, Bakery, and Market. Today the owner manages all orders manually through Instagram DMs and WhatsApp, tracks inventory in a notebook, and has no system to accept online payments.

## Pain Point

"I lose orders because people message me on Instagram and WhatsApp and I forget to follow up. I have no way to take payments online, I never know which products are selling, and I spend more time answering messages than making food."

## User Roles

- Owner: administrator who manages products, orders, and notifications
- Customer: anyone who browses and purchases ALMA products online

## Solution

A cloud-hosted e-commerce platform where customers browse and purchase ALMA products online, and the owner receives automatic order notifications without manual follow-up.

## Success Measure

The pain point is gone when customers complete a purchase online without contacting the owner directly, and every order is automatically saved and confirmed by email.
